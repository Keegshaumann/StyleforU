Our Story

A salon built around how you want to feel.

Tucked into Retail Crossing on Hendrik Potgieter Road, Style 4 U is a warm, considered space where the focus is simple: hair that makes you feel like yourself — only a little more so.

From bespoke colour and luminous balayage to expert braiding, extensions and smoothing treatments, every service is tailored — unhurried, attentive, and finished with care.
